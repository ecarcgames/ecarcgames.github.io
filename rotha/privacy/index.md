# Privacy Policy for Rotha

**Last updated: 19 September 2026**

Rotha: Color Logic Puzzle ("Rotha") is a mobile puzzle game developed and published by EC Arc Games ("we", "us" or "our").

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Rotha, including information processed by third-party advertising services.

For privacy questions, contact us at [ecarcgames@gmail.com](mailto:ecarcgames@gmail.com).

## 1. Information stored on your device

Rotha stores certain information locally on your device to provide its gameplay features. This may include:

* Campaign progress, completed levels, scores and earned stars.
* Daily puzzle results, streaks and related progress.
* Clear mode progress and gameplay statistics.
* Achievements, game settings and preferences.
* Advertising consent preferences and notification settings, where applicable.

This information is used to operate the game and preserve your progress and preferences.

Rotha does not require you to create an account. We do not operate a cloud-save service or intentionally transmit your locally stored puzzle progress to our own servers.

Uninstalling the game or clearing its application data may permanently remove locally stored progress and preferences.

## 2. Advertising and third-party data processing

Rotha uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including banner advertisements and optional rewarded advertisements.

Google and its advertising partners may process information relating to your device and use of the app to deliver advertisements, measure advertising performance, prevent fraud and support advertising services.

Depending on your device, location, consent choices and applicable law, this information may include:

* Advertising identifiers and other device or app identifiers.
* IP address and approximate location derived from it.
* Device information, operating system and app information.
* Advertising interactions, diagnostic information and information used for fraud prevention.

Advertising data may be processed by Google and its advertising partners according to their applicable privacy policies and your consent choices.

For more information, see:

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Google advertising controls: https://myadcenter.google.com/

## 3. Advertising consent and privacy choices

Where required, Rotha uses Google's consent management tools to request and manage advertising consent.

You may be presented with choices concerning personalised advertising and related data processing.

Where available, you can revisit applicable advertising consent choices through the game's Settings menu under Privacy Choices.

Your choices may affect the advertisements you receive but will not prevent you from accessing Rotha's core puzzle gameplay.

You may also manage certain advertising preferences through your device settings and Google's advertising controls.

## 4. Optional rewarded advertisements

Rotha offers optional rewarded advertisements that may provide gameplay benefits, such as additional moves or access to certain tools.

Watching a rewarded advertisement is voluntary. You can continue using the game's core features without watching rewarded advertisements, although some optional benefits may remain unavailable.

Google AdMob processes information associated with advertisement delivery and interactions as described in Section 2.

## 5. Notifications

Rotha may request permission to send optional local notifications, such as reminders about Daily puzzles.

These notifications are scheduled on your device. You can disable them through your device's notification settings.

Rotha does not require notification permission to play the game.

## 6. Sharing

Rotha allows you to share certain game results using compatible sharing features on your device.

Sharing is initiated by you. If you choose to share a result through another application or service, the information you share will be handled according to that service's privacy practices.

Rotha does not require you to share results to access its core gameplay.

## 7. Children's privacy

Rotha is intended for teenagers aged 13 and older and adults. It is not specifically directed at children under 13.

We do not knowingly request personal information directly from children under 13 through an account registration system or other in-game personal information form.

Third-party advertising services may process information as described in this Privacy Policy.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us directly, please contact [ecarcgames@gmail.com](mailto:ecarcgames@gmail.com).

## 8. Data retention and deletion

Game progress and preferences stored locally on your device may remain until you clear the application's data or uninstall Rotha.

Advertising-related information processed by Google and its partners is subject to their respective retention policies and applicable legal requirements.

To request information about personal data that EC Arc Games may hold directly, contact [ecarcgames@gmail.com](mailto:ecarcgames@gmail.com).

For information about data processed by Google, consult Google's privacy policy and privacy controls.

## 9. Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect information within the services we control.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Information processed by third-party services is subject to their security practices.

## 10. International data processing

Google and its advertising partners may process information in countries other than your country of residence.

Such processing is governed by their applicable privacy policies, contractual arrangements and relevant legal requirements.

## 11. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to Rotha, its features, third-party services or applicable legal requirements.

When we make changes, we will update the date at the top of this policy.
